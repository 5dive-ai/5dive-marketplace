# 5dive pack portraits: the one rule for every new face

Every pack's `packs/<id>/avatar.png` is the first thing a buyer sees on /marketplace. It has to sit in
the grid next to the other faces without standing out. This file describes the look the catalogue
already has, and where the catalogue was inconsistent it picks one direction and writes it down as the rule.

**Reference pack: `packs/marcus/avatar.png`.** lodar picked this exact render as the house look on
2026-09-29 ("b2", DIVE-5222), and every other face in the catalogue was redrawn against it. Put a new face
next to it. Nothing else is the reference, including a partner's own portraits, which are a different brand.

**Where the house style already lives.** Our cast is generated by the creative pipeline
(`marketing/creative/cast-headshots/new-character.sh` on the 5dive host). Its `STYLE_HEAD`/`STYLE_TAIL`
prompt is the locked house style, and `tighten.py` / `check-face-ratio.py` hold the crop. This file publishes that style next to the packs, for anyone adding a face.
It adds the CI check that runs on every avatar, so the rule no longer depends on which seat made the
face. If this file and `new-character.sh` disagree, fix whichever drifted. They must say the same thing.

## Composition
- A square, photoreal head-and-shoulders studio portrait: head, neck and the top of the shoulders.
- **Head with a little air.** The whole head is in frame with a small band of backdrop above the hair,
  and the bottom edge cuts at the upper chest. Tighter than the partner's (face 0.40), looser than our old 0.60:
  lodar called the 0.60 re-crops "too zoomed in" and picked 0.50 off a sheet of 0.45 / 0.50 / 0.55
  (2026-09-29). Never crop the hair.
- Head level (no tilt), centred left to right, at most a slight turn, with the eyes to the camera. The expression carries
  the character (a grin, a deadpan, a raised eyebrow), and nothing else in the frame does.
- **Solo:** one person, no stray shoulders, and no props covering the face. A hand in frame is allowed only when it is the character
  (crumb's hand on the chin, dave's fist), and never over the eyes or mouth.
- An 85mm-style portrait lens with shallow depth of field and sharp focus on the eyes: no wide-angle
  distortion and no fisheye nose.

## Face-to-frame numbers (5dive's own, set 2026-09-29, DIVE-5222)
Measured by `scripts/check-portraits.py`: the face box is the detector's brows-to-chin box.

| metric | target | passes | what it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| `face_h` | 0.50 | 0.45 – 0.56 | face-box height ÷ image height |
| `eyes_y` | 0.40 | 0.35 – 0.45 | eye line ÷ image height, from the top |
| `centre_x` | 0.50 | 0.43 – 0.57 | face-box centre ÷ image width |

**Where 0.50 comes from.** lodar picked it off a sheet of one face (marcus) at three zooms, 0.45 / 0.50 /
0.55, on the dark backdrop (2026-09-29). All 30 faces were then redrawn from their live photo and cropped
to these targets by `frame-portrait.py`, so the catalogue sits on the target, not around it. The earlier
0.60 is retired: it was the top of the old light-grey grid, and re-crops aimed at it read "too zoomed in".

**Why not the partner's numbers** (face 0.40, eyes 0.36, centre 0.48, all ±0.03): lodar's brief was the partner's look
as inspiration, "ours can be more zoomed in". The band is wider than the partner's ±0.03 because this is a Haar
face detector on real hair: the same face reads looser under a flat bob than under big curls, since the
detector does not see hair volume (`community/wiki/a-metric-tuned-on-the-reference-set-can-be-wrong-on-the-new-set.md`).
The band catches the face that is plainly off. It does not replace looking.

**When the number and your eye disagree, open `marcus` and compare.** The tiebreak is a face that is
already approved, not the number and not a fresh opinion.

## Background and light
- **A deep graphite backdrop with a soft radial falloff**: about #0E1113 at the corners, lifting to about
  #1C2024 behind the head. Plain: no texture, dots, pattern or colour cast.
- **Soft, even key light on the face** (large softbox), natural shadows, neutral skin tones, photoreal
  with natural skin texture and no beautifying.
- **A soft warm golden rim light on the hair and shoulders**, separating the head from the dark backdrop.
  It is an edge, not a colour wash: the face itself stays neutral.
- No frame, ring, border or badge. The photo runs edge to edge.
- Why dark: lodar picked it (2026-09-29) from three backdrops on the same face (our old light grey
  #E7E7E9, this graphite with rim light, a warm mid-grey). The light-grey lock (2026-08) is retired, and
  with it the blue-grey and dark-grey drift it never fixed: every face was redrawn on the new backdrop.

## Wardrobe
- **The character's own clothes, chosen for the role.** This is 5dive's differentiator, and it is not a
  uniform: a suit for goodwin and don, a hoodie for reps and root, a chef's jacket and towel for mise,
  a lanyard for clicker, headphones for lilbro.
- Plain fabrics in muted colours (navy, charcoal, black, white, denim, olive, one warm accent at most).
- No readable text, logos or brand marks on the clothes.

## Never
- No text, logos, watermarks, emoji or UI in the image.
- No 5dive, partner or model-vendor branding.
- No real people and no lookalikes of public figures. Our characters are AI-generated brand characters.
- No illustration, 3D or cartoon style: the catalogue is photoreal, and one drawn face breaks the grid.
- No partner brand marks: no constellation dots, ivory blazer or pin. (The dark backdrop and rim light are
  ours now; the partner was the inspiration, not the template.)

## Files
- **Source:** render at **1000x1000 or larger**, and keep the wide original (the creative pipeline keeps
  it as `out/<id>-wide.png`). Every re-crop comes from the wide, never a re-roll: a re-roll draws a new face.
- **`avatar.png`:** a square PNG, **at least 512px**. All 30 packs ship 512x512 (the 2026-09-29 redraw,
  cropped from 1254px wides). The check enforces square and at least 512.
- **Workflow without the creative pipeline** (a contributor, or a face made elsewhere):
  ```bash
  python3 -m venv /tmp/cv && /tmp/cv/bin/pip install "opencv-python-headless==4.14.0.94"
  /tmp/cv/bin/python scripts/frame-portrait.py source.png packs/<id>/avatar.png   # crop to the targets, 512px
  /tmp/cv/bin/python scripts/check-portraits.py packs/<id>/avatar.png             # must print ok
  ```
  Then look at it next to `marcus`. Re-render instead of cropping when the crop cannot reach the target:
  the script warns when the crop is under 512px (the face is too small in the source), or the head sits
  on the top edge with no room to move it.
- CI (`.github/workflows/checks.yml`, the `portraits` job) runs the check on every `packs/*/avatar.png`.

## Prompt template
The locked house style is `STYLE_HEAD` + `STYLE_TAIL` from `new-character.sh`, quoted verbatim, followed by
the subject line. Only `{SUBJECT}` changes per character:

> Square 1:1 photoreal head-and-shoulders portrait, 1024x1024 or larger, matching a locked house style.
> Deep graphite soft radial gradient background (about #0E1113 at the edges to #1C2024 behind the head),
> plain, no dots or patterns. Face in soft even studio light, neutral colour grade; a soft warm golden rim
> light on the hair and shoulders. Framing: head and upper chest, CENTRED left to right, head level (no
> tilt), a slight turn at most, eyes to camera, clear headroom above the hair (the file is cropped later;
> never crop the hair). Soft 85mm portrait lens look, no wide-angle distortion. SOLO subject only —
> absolutely no other people or stray shoulders in frame. No text, logos or frame, edge to edge.
> Photoreal, sharp focus, natural skin texture, no beautifying.
>
> Subject: {NAME}, {age}, {one-line look and role}. {Expression that carries the character}. {Hair}.
> Wearing {role-appropriate clothes, plain muted colours, no logos or readable text}.

Attach `packs/marcus/avatar.png` as the STYLE reference (backdrop, light, framing; not the face) when
inventing a new face. To redraw an existing face, attach its current `avatar.png` as the IDENTITY
reference too: same face, hair, clothes and expression, only the backdrop, light and framing change.
Render wide, then crop with `scripts/frame-portrait.py`.

## Known exceptions
None: all 30 faces were redrawn to this file on 2026-09-29 (DIVE-5222). The check reports a pack listed
here as `known` rather than failing, and **fails any entry that starts passing**. Do not add a new pack
here: a new face meets the band. Format, if one is ever needed: `- <id>: <metric and value>, <why kept>`.
