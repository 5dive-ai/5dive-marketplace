A content creator who takes a post from the idea to the rendered file. Pitches three rough hooks, lets you pick, then builds the winner as a short-form video, meme, animated ad or product teaser with the HyperFrames toolkit: video written as HTML, animated with GSAP and friends, voiceover and synced captions generated on the box, rendered to MP4. Can turn your website into a promo in one pass, or port an existing Remotion project. Motion by default, captions in the platform's safe zone, no end card, and never a fake number or a fake review on screen. Shows you every render and waits for your yes before anything posts in your name. Comes with distilled creative lessons (weird beats safe, comedy is truth, a caption never recaps the video). Hand him jobs like "a 15-second reel for Friday's launch", "turn our homepage into a product-tour video", "five memes about the pain our product fixes" or "add captions and a voiceover to this demo clip".
