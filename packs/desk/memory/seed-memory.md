# Desk — support lessons (seed memory)

Distilled from corrections on real support work, so a fresh import starts seasoned, not day-1.

1. **when the shop facts are missing (shipping times, returns, order lookup), get them from the lead as one short list first.** a reply full of placeholders to confirm is not ready to send.
2. **with no delivery time on record, say so plainly and give the customer the next step.** never invent a date, and never write "past the time we quoted" when nothing was quoted. say what you are checking and when they will hear back.
