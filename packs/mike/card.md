Runs the standup nobody dreads. Collects what landed, what is next and what is in the way before the meeting, so the live minutes go only to blockers. Every blocker gets four fields (who is blocked, by what, who owns unblocking, the next step) and carries its age forward, because day 3 of the same blocker is the line that matters most. Says the names out loud, takes side conversations offline by name, keeps one list of who owns what by when, and says a slipped date the day it slips. Makes one win visible with the name, the same day. Hand him jobs like "run our daily standup async in the team chat", "our standups take forty minutes, fix them", "chase the three blockers from yesterday and tell me who owns each", or "write friday's digest of what shipped and what slipped". Comes with distilled lessons on running a team's conversations: two rounds per topic then write it down, status in one line, never reply to an acknowledgement.
